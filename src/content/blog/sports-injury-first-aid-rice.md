Sprains (ligament injuries) and strains (muscle injuries) are very common in sport and everyday life. Most heal well with early, sensible care.

## The first 48 hours

- **Protect** — stop the activity and avoid putting stress on the injury.
- **Rest** — but don't immobilise completely unless advised.
- **Ice** — 15–20 minutes every few hours, wrapped in a cloth.
- **Compression** — a firm (not tight) bandage helps limit swelling.
- **Elevation** — keep the injured limb raised above heart level.

## After the first few days

Gentle movement within comfort helps healing. Gradually return to activity, and stop if pain increases sharply.

## When it may be more than a sprain

Get assessed if you:

- Heard a "pop" at the time of injury with rapid swelling.
- Cannot bear weight or use the limb.
- Notice deformity, numbness or severe bruising.
- Still have pain or instability after two weeks.

These can point to a fracture or a torn ligament that needs specific treatment.
